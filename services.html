01 Our specialty

Whole-home repiping

Old galvanized, polybutylene or leaking copper supply lines, replaced with PEX or copper in one planned job.

What we do

A walk-through of the house and a written quote first. Then new supply lines, new shut-off valves, and a pressure test before the walls are closed.

Signs you need it

  • Rusty or discolored water
  • Low pressure on several fixtures
  • Pinhole leaks that keep coming back
  • Original pipes from before 1990
Free repipe estimate or call (360) 318-6219

Not sure your pipes need it yet? Book a free check-up: we look and tell you what we find.

02

Drain cleaning

Sinks, tubs, showers, toilets and main lines.

What we do

We clear the clog properly, with no chemicals poured down the pipe, and explain why it clogged.

Signs you need it

  • Slow drains
  • Gurgling sounds
  • A sewer smell
  • Water coming back up
Free drain estimate or call (360) 318-6219

Not sure it is only the drain? Book a free check-up. No charge, no pressure.

03

Water heaters

Tank and tankless. Repair when it makes sense, replace when it does not.

What we do

We check the unit first and tell you plainly whether a repair is worth it. If it needs replacing, we install the new one and haul the old unit away.

Signs you need it

  • No hot water, or it runs out fast
  • Water around the base of the tank
  • Rumbling noises
  • Rusty hot water
Free water heater estimate or call (360) 318-6219

Not sure a repair is worth it? Book a free check-up. No charge, no pressure.

04

Leak detection & repair

Walls, ceilings, floors and slabs.

What we do

We find the leak first and open only what is needed to reach it. Then we repair the line and test it.

Signs you need it

  • A water bill higher than usual
  • Damp spots or a musty smell
  • Running water sounds with every tap off
Free leak estimate or call (360) 318-6219

Not sure there is a leak? Book a free check-up. No charge, no pressure.

05

Repairs & fixtures

Faucets, sinks, toilets, garbage disposals, shut-off valves and hose bibs. Small jobs welcome.

What we do

We fix what is there, or install the new fixture you picked out yourself.

Signs you need it

  • A faucet that drips or a toilet that keeps running
  • A shut-off valve that will not turn
  • A leaking hose bib
  • A new faucet or disposal still in the box
Free repair estimate or call (360) 318-6219

Want the rest of the plumbing looked at too? Book a free check-up. No charge.
